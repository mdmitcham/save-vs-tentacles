A play report / stat block.

The sun hangs low and heavy out past the breaking waves and gleams red over the low hills of that contested scrubland. The stones here are salty and quiet and dry and jealous of that secret being called Jimithus. For he is old as they and knows them as they know him. He has gone on before the coming of the four great cities and will go on after.

A low shaded cavern is his home. Cool in the heat of the day and warm at its ending. And in the walls of his home are handprints in clay and ash of those first men. And in the cavern is gold also. For no bright thing lay long where Jimithus had seen it. And in those days he had stolen the great treasure of Aethonia and left its vaults empty as the ancient halls of those first men.

In those days also, a band of heroes bearing Aethonian crests rode out to treat with him, for they had heard that he was a cunning creature and capable also. And indeed they rode up into that rocky scrubland and beheld the hills that closed like a fist and said, Here must be Jimithus the Dancing.

Thus to attract him they took pieces of metal and silver and twisted jewelry which were gifts from the Satyrs and hung them from trees. They built a fire of pine and oak limbs so that meager wealth glittered and flashed in its light. And here too did the muses strike them for they mimed ancient dances which were known in the old days. And here did Jimithus the Dancing come upon them and danced with them and spoke of many ancient things and discussed grave secrets.

Yet upon the morning they wept, for they had lost their knowing in the revelry and knew it would not come to them again, and the salt of their tears became as pillars.

Also, the jewelry was gone.

And behold, they tracked Jimithus a day toward his cave in a deep ravine and saw there that he had much wealth. And here they knelt and asked would he come and live in the vaults and the great gardens of Aethonia and dance there with the King's fireflies and among the satyrs.

And Jimithus agreed for he knew futures beyond reckoning and also had rolled a 10 on his reaction roll.

And so they invited him to be held and swaddled him. And they bore him from that cave with the treasures back to Aethonia and set him there in the garden, in that king's garden which had grown for centuries under the light of Apollyon, and made for him a home in the vaults also.

And there was great merriment and breaking of bread and feasting. So did the Queen Alcimenis proclaim him Jimithus the Rich.

Yet still did our heroes brood and mutter. They could not know what secrets had opened to them in that first firelit revel, and the shades of their future pulled them on and never back. Nor would their hearts bear the courage to return.

Yet in the night Jimithus said that the hearts of those first men beat on, and the ash and clay paintings were and will be again. And still he dances there in the gardens and in the vaults among the gold of Aethonia. He is dancing, dancing. He says that he will never die.

## Jimithus the Dancing (Small)

**A1, 6GD, VIG 15, CLA 15, SPI 5**

- **Claw** (d10), Leaping Tackle
- **Salt of Denial**: Jimithus negates the first hit against him each round.
- **Charm**: 1d6 creatures must pass a CLA save or treat Jimithus as a friend.

If Small Jimithus is Mortally Wounded, within 1 round he becomes Large Jimithus.

## Jimithus the Dancing (Large)

**A3 (thick hide), 14GD, VIG 19, CLA 17, SPI 15. Fights as a Warband.**

- **Ripping claws** (3d10)
- **Horrifying Swallow** (slow): a creature must pass a VIG save or be swallowed, taking 2d12 damage and becoming Impaired.
